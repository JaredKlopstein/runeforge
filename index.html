How to Play

Gather with skilled villagers, level their skills through use, raise a base through the Bronze → Iron → Rune ages, and raze the enemy Town Center before they raze yours.

A good opening

  1. Let Auto work. Villagers start on Auto: they fish, chop and mine what you are short on, build what you place, and run into the Town Center when raiders come. Press . to find anyone idle.
  2. Grow. Select the Town Center and press V a few times. Select villagers and press H to place a House before the population bar fills.
  3. Feed. Press J for a Farm and put one villager on it. Fish run out; farms do not.
  4. Arm. Press B for a Barracks. Select it and press N for a Scout (it explores by itself) and G for Guards. The top bar counts down to the next enemy army.
  5. Age up. When the Town Center's Advance button lights, take the Iron Age for Rangers, Rams, Towers and the Forge. Upgrade buildings with U as Construction rises.

On a touch screen

Tap a unit or building to select it. With units selected, tap a tree, fish, building or the ground to give the same order a right-click gives. Drag with one finger to pan, pinch to zoom, hold a finger on anything for its tooltip. The toolbar on the right gives you box-select, add-to-selection, attack-move, next idle villager, select army, and jump home. Placing a building: tap to position it, tap the same spot again to confirm.
